Why are my leaves yellow?

Asked last spring · 41 answers

A pot plant by the window has turned yellow from the bottom up over two weeks. It is watered twice a week.

Top answer

Check in this order: overwatering, low light, then root rot.

Twice a week is often too much. Let the top few centimetres of soil dry out before watering again. If the plant is far from the window, move it closer. If the soil smells sour or the roots are brown and soft, repot it in fresh soil and cut the damaged roots away.
