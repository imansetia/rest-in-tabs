Lighthouses of Portugal

Six lighthouses on the coast of Portugal, from the Berlengas islands off Peniche down to Sagres at the south-west corner. The table gives the year each present tower was first lit.

LighthouseRegionBuilt
Farol da GuiaCascais1761
Cabo da RocaSintra1772
Cabo CarvoeiroPeniche1790
Cabo EspichelSesimbra1790
BerlengaBerlengas islands1841
Cabo de São VicenteSagres1846

Several of the towers stand on the sites of older lights.
