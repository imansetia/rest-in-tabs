Sourdough starter: a beginner's guide

Baking basics · 8 minute read

A starter is flour and water that has been left to ferment until wild yeast and bacteria settle in. Fed regularly, it raises bread without any packet yeast.

Daily schedule

DayMorningEvening
1Mix 50 g wholemeal flour with 50 g waterStir, cover loosely
2 to 4Discard half, add 50 g flour and 50 g waterStir
5 to 7Discard half, feed twice a dayMark the level on the jar

Storing and reviving

Between bakes the starter keeps in the fridge for up to two weeks. To revive it, leave it at room temperature for an hour, then give it two feeds twelve hours apart. It is ready when it doubles within six hours.
